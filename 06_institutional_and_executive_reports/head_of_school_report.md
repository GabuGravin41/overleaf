# EV Charging Infrastructure Project Proposal
**Prepared for:** Head of School, African Olympiad Academy (Kanombe, Kigali)
**Subject:** Implementation of Charging Station for 2x Golden Dragon Electric Buses (XML6559JEV)

---

## 1. Executive Summary
The school is acquiring two Golden Dragon XML6559JEV electric 15-seater buses (53 kWh batteries). To operate these efficiently, we must install a dedicated on-site charging station. This report outlines the technical requirements, the application process with EUCL (Energy Utility Corporation Limited), required documentation, and a project timeline with estimated costs.

## 2. Technical Scope & Strategy
After analyzing the bus specifications and expected usage (morning/afternoon routes with long midday/overnight idle times), we recommend installing **two 22 kW AC Chargers (Type 2, 3-Phase)** rather than expensive DC Fast Chargers.

*   **Charging Time:** ~2.5 to 3 hours from 0% to 100%.
*   **Grid Impact:** Requests an additional 44 kW of power from the local grid.
*   **Cost Efficiency:** AC chargers are significantly cheaper to purchase and install, and place less strain on the Kanombe sector transformer.

## 3. The EUCL Capacity Upgrade Application
Because this new infrastructure requires an additional 44 kW of power, we must formally apply to EUCL for a **Commercial Capacity Upgrade**. 

### 3.1 Why a Physical Application is Superior to Online
While EUCL offers an online portal, we strongly recommend submitting this application **in person** at the Kicukiro EUCL branch for the following reasons:
1.  **Complexity of Request:** EV charging is non-standard infrastructure. An online application may be miscategorized as a standard commercial upgrade and delayed. Speaking directly with the branch engineer ensures they understand the technical load profile of electric buses.
2.  **Establishing a Point of Contact:** By going in person, we secure the direct phone number of the EUCL site surveyor assigned to Kanombe, allowing us to actively follow up and expedite the site visit.
3.  **Immediate Issue Resolution:** Any discrepancies in the school's documentation or technical load requests can be clarified on the spot, preventing multi-day delays caused by online rejections.

### 3.2 Required Documentation Checklist
To submit the application, we require the following documents from the school's administration. *(Note: EUCL requires these to verify the legal standing and property rights of the institution).*

- [ ] **School Registration Certificate:** Official proof of the African Olympiad Academy's legal registration.
- [ ] **Land Title (UPI):** Proof of ownership for the school's plot in Kanombe.
- [ ] **Legal Representative ID:** A photocopy of the National ID or Passport of the Head of School (or the legally authorized signatory).
- [ ] **Letter of Authorization:** A formal letter on school letterhead, signed and stamped by the Head of School, authorizing the engineering student to apply for the capacity upgrade and act as the technical liaison for the project.
- [ ] **Technical Equipment List:** (Prepared by the engineering student) Detailing the 2x 22kW chargers and the 44 kW load request.

> [!TIP]
> **Decision for the Head of School:** 
> You do not *need* to go to the EUCL office in person. If you prefer to save time, you can simply provide the four documents listed above (including the signed Authorization Letter), and the engineering student can execute the application and handle all technical conversations with the EUCL engineers on your behalf.

## 4. End-to-End Project Roadmap & Timeline

The project will be executed in four phases over an estimated 4 to 8 weeks.

### Phase 1: Application & Site Survey (Weeks 1-3)
*   Gather required documents from school administration.
*   Submit physical application to EUCL Kicukiro branch.
*   EUCL engineers visit the school to assess the main electrical panel and the local Kanombe transformer.
*   EUCL approves the capacity upgrade (and quotes a fee if a transformer upgrade is required).

### Phase 2: Sourcing & Procurement (Weeks 2-4)
*   Contact authorized local EV infrastructure suppliers in Kigali (e.g., CEVR, Kabisa, IZI Electric).
*   Obtain competitive quotes for two 22 kW AC chargers, including RURA-certified installation.
*   School administration approves the final vendor quote.

### Phase 3: Site Preparation (Weeks 4-6)
*   Select the exact parking location for the buses (ideally close to the main electrical panel to save on cabling costs).
*   Execute civil works: digging cable trenches, pouring concrete pedestals for the chargers, and optionally building a rain/sun canopy.

### Phase 4: Installation & Commissioning (Weeks 7-8)
*   Vendor installs chargers and runs heavy-duty 3-phase cabling.
*   System testing and safety checks (grounding, surge protection).
*   Staff training on how to safely plug in and monitor the Golden Dragon buses.
