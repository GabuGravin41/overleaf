\section*{2. Detailed Block Descriptions}

\subsection*{2.1 Customer Segments \& Total Addressable Market}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Primary Segment}: Over 460,000 university students in Kenya living off-campus (82\% of 560,000 total enrolled students across public and private universities).
    \item \textbf{Secondary Segment}: Over 22,000 private hostel owners, independent landlords, and caretakers seeking rapid room occupancy and automated rent collection.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{2.2 Value Proposition \& Moat}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Zero Financial Pressure for Landlords}: Landlords list 1 property for free. This guarantees fast inventory aggregation across university nodes.
    \item \textbf{Student Anti-Fraud Guarantee}: Funds are locked safely in escrow and only released 48 hours after key receipt.
    \item \textbf{Infrastructure Lock-in}: Once landlords manage room inventory and tenant repair requests on HomeBase, switching costs become prohibitively high.
\end{itemize}
