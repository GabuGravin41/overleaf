\section{Market Data \& Baseline Assumptions}

The financial model is constructed using empirical data from the \textbf{Commission for University Education (CUE)} and \textbf{Kenya National Bureau of Statistics (KNBS)}:

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item \textbf{Total Enrolled University Students in Kenya}: 560,000 students.
    \item \textbf{Off-Campus Student Housing Deficit}: 82\% (460,000 students).
    \item \textbf{Average Monthly Rent per Student Room}: KSh 8,500.
    \item \textbf{Average Building Capacity}: 12 room units per landlord property.
    \item \textbf{Average Room Occupancy Rate}: 85\% (accounting for semester turnarounds).
\end{itemize}
